# Palette Pal
Palette Pal is a Asesprite extensions for making Palettes from a single colour. 
## Features
Palette Pal Currently has 5 Palettes and a tool for inverting your Fore/Background Colours. Each feature is avaliable for keyboard shortcuts or use via a Dialog menu. 
### Palettes 
- **Analogous** Your colour and 2 similar colours.
- **Monochromatic** Your colour and 8 shades.
- **Split Complementary** Your colour and 2 similar colours to the colour oppisite of your colour.
- **Tradic** Your colour wheel and 2 colours 120° from your colour.
- **Quadratic** Like tradic but a 4 points 90° from each other.

## Setup
1. Download the latest version from [here](https://github.com/DabbiSabbi/Palette-Pal/releases)
2. Open Aseprite
3. Hover over edit and select "Preferences" at the bottom of the dropdown.
4. Click extensions on the side bar.
5. Click add extensions and click on "Palette Pal Extension.aseprite-extension" and click "OK".
6. Hover over edit again and click "Keyboard Shortcuts"
7. Search for "Dialog" and set a shortcut for "Palette Pal: Open Palette Pal Dialog" and click "OK". This shortcut will open the menu.

Enjoy!

All the options in the menu can also be used with shortcuts, all shortcuts from this extension have "Palette Pal:" at the start.
